Keeping Bees on a Balcony

A balcony four floors up is not where most people picture a hive, yet the bees on Corvin Street have done well there for three summers. The trick, their keeper says, is to think about wind before anything else.

Choosing the spot

Bees want morning sun and shelter from the gusts that sweep between buildings. A corner with a solid wall on two sides is ideal, and a screen of tall grasses helps the rest.

Keep the entrance facing away from the door you use, so that you and the foragers are not always in each other's way.

What you will need

  • A small hive with a sloped roof
  • A shallow water dish with stones in it
  • Plants that flower from spring to autumn

Keeping records

Write down what you see at every inspection. A plain text file works as well as any app, as long as you keep it in one place.

date: 12 May
brood: good
temper: calm
Bees forgive a slow keeper, but never a clumsy one.

By the end of the first season the hive gave nine jars of honey, which went to the neighbours who had put up with the buzzing.
